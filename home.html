Our Story

A Decade of Devotion to the Art of Taste

AURUM was born from a singular obsession: to create a space where dining transcends necessity and becomes memory. Founded in 2012 by Chef Aryan Mehta, our kitchen has earned recognition that spans continents.

Every evening at AURUM is an eight-course conversation between our kitchen and your senses. We believe that great dining is the highest form of hospitality — a choreography of flavour, beauty and human connection.
