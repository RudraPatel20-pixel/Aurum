AURUM — Latin for gold — was conceived as more than a restaurant. It was to be a temple of taste; a place where a single meal could become a chapter in someone's life story.

Chef Aryan Mehta
Founder & Executive Chef
The Architect of Flavour

Trained by the World's
Greatest Kitchens

Born in Jaipur and trained at Le Cordon Bleu Paris, Chef Aryan spent formative years under Alain Ducasse at Louis XV in Monaco and Heston Blumenthal at The Fat Duck before returning to India with a singular vision.

His cuisine honours the subcontinent's extraordinary larder through a French lens — never fusion, always translation. Each dish is a dialogue between memory and possibility.
